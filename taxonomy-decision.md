# Taxonomy decision — 2026-09-07

Agriculture, Academia & Research, and Culture remain tags and editorial topics,
not new publication sections.

The current section model already routes innovation, science and medicine,
technology and AI, achievements, international cooperation, people, courage
and service, and history/context to **The People of Israel**. The hub presents
those records together while `topicTags` provide the finer subject vocabulary.
Adding three first-class sections would split a small body of work into sparse
destination pages without a routing, homepage, or reader-journey need.

Revisit this only when one subject has enough recurring, distinct records to
justify its own hub, homepage treatment, and editorial ownership. Until then,
use a precise tag plus the existing People section.
